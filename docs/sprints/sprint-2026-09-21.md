# Sprint 2026-09-21 — Prove the product before touching anything else

**Revision 2, 2026-09-18.** This supersedes revision 1 of this file in
place (opened by PR #5, merged 2026-09-18). Revision 1 planned five site
items. The owner reviewed that plan and rejected its triage: recorded as
incident 12 (docs/agents/incidents.md) and all-hands decision 11
(docs/allhands/2026-09-17.md, 2026-09-18). Her critique, in substance:
the product is the content, and the content is thin and unproven, roughly
two digest issues with no testing or validation of them, and two skills,
one of them never validated. Site plumbing led the plan while that stayed
untouched. Her release gate: nothing releases honestly until the product
scores a five on the OKR benchmark, judged against the market agent's
comparison set (docs/okrs/okrs-2026-Q4.md's five axes: speed, judgment,
reader actionability, agent actionability, surface). She also named two
non-negotiables for release regardless of the score: a real domain, and
a UI with no coming-soon pages standing in for something being sold.

This revision re-triages around that gate. Digest quality and skills-
repository work rank above everything else this week. Site plumbing
keeps only what that quality work itself needs to be visible; two items
from revision 1 (email capture, the live hero metric) are cut and
carried, since neither moves the score. See docs/sprints/pending.md for
the three things this revision owes the owner directly rather than
planning around: the release-gate reconciliation (Oct 13 conditional, or
a phased launch, both costed), the domain as a dated card for her
purchase, and the coming-soon audit.

This still serves O1 (the benchmark and launch objective) and O2 (the
skill library as a real asset), and it still runs inside the 2026-10-13
launch runway the 2026-09-17 all-hands set. It does not serve O1 by
building more site around a thin product; it serves O1 by making what is
already built worth what it claims to be.
