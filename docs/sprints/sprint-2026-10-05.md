## Definition of done (owner's goal, PM's criteria)

The owner's own words on the item: papers in, claims distilled, the
Monday issue printed and delivered to real subscribers from a verified
domain, subscribe and unsubscribe on the site, so that no human step is
anywhere in the week; working means a person can use it end to end
today; autonomous means it runs on its own triggers and the bus with
nobody in the loop, and when it fails a seat triages it.

Operationalized, an item only counts as done against this sprint when
all of the following hold, because each clause above has a concrete,
checkable artifact:

1. **A stranger, today, with no human touching a file or a table,**
   can submit their email on the site and become a real row in
   `subscribers` (comped is fine; paid is Phase 2). Not a waitlist a
   person has to be promoted out of by hand.
2. **A subscriber can unsubscribe from the site**, as a request that
   flips their own row, not a reply-to-this-email the owner has to read
   and act on.
3. **The Monday send happens on its own clock and reaches the inbox**
   it was sent to, from whatever sending path is live (Gmail SMTP
   today; moving to a verified domain is Phase 2 and costs money, which
   is the owner's call, named below rather than assumed).
4. **A failed step raises to a seat, not only to the owner's inbox.**
   Today's only alarm path mails the owner directly; that is a human
   step by the DoD's own definition, named below as a gap rather than
   silently accepted.
5. Everything upstream of send (ingest, distill, triage, interpret)
   keeps running unattended, which is already true and is not
   reopened by this regrooming.

**What serves this and stays. What does not, moves to next.**

## What still stands between the press and a week with no human step

Four things, each named against a file rather than an impression, in
the order they block the DoD above:

1. **Subscribing is a waitlist, not a subscription** (item 2). A
   person who signs up today becomes a line in a JSONL file that does
   not survive a redeploy, not a `subscribers` row. Someone has to
   promote them by hand. This is the single clearest gap against
   "a person can use it end to end today."
2. **Unsubscribing is an email reply, not a site action** (item 3). The
   owner is the unsubscribe mechanism today, by the pipeline's own
   comment.
3. **Sending is the owner's personal Gmail, not a verified domain**,
   by design, at Phase 1 scale (`docs/vision.md`: "the cron sends
   through the owner's own Gmail via authenticated SMTP... at this
   scale Gmail's sender reputation IS the deliverability strategy").
   Phase 2 (buy a domain, SES, SPF/DKIM) is named there already and is
   not a sprint item: it spends money and changes a paid service,
   which this seat's charter puts in the owner's hands, not a seat's.
   **Needs your call**, not a dispatch.
4. **A failure's first reader is the owner, not a seat.** Guardrail 3's
   only alarm channel (`pipeline/notify.py`) mails the owner over the
   same Gmail path. No seat currently receives or triages that alert.
   Closing this is bigger than a day and is named here as a gap for a
   future sprint, not assigned today.

`main`'s current red state (item 1) is a temporary blocker on shipping
items 2 and 3 cleanly, not a design gap in the autonomy itself, and is
already being worked.
