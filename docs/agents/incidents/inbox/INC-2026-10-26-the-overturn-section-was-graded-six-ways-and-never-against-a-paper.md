## INC-2026-10-26-the-overturn-section-was-graded-six-ways-and-never-against-a-paper - a complete sixteen-law grade of a section that was wrong three for three, because the procedure had no question about sources (2026-10-26, writer seat)

**What happened.** The research seat read the live `2026-W42`'s overturn section
against the two abstracts that section cites and scored it **three wrong out of
three** (`docs/research/notes/2026-10-26-w42-left-behind-audit.md`, merged
2026-10-26). One statement calls a result overturned when it is the citing
paper's own proven theorem. One attaches a 93% figure and a timing side-channel
mechanism to "the same paper", which is a theory paper containing neither. One
reports Vosti as contradicted by Vosti, where the two halves are that paper's
own two findings about two different systems.

Writer run 51 graded that same section the same morning, nine hours earlier. It
ran all sixteen canon laws and the six grading passes, it found four real
defects in the section, and it reported the section's prose. Its law 9 verdict
names "Vosti had claimed bitwise-identical logits" as an attribution shape and
gives the repair for the shape. The sentence is also false, and nothing in the
procedure asked.

**Why it is in this register, and it is not the obvious repeat.** The obvious
one is the class: `2026-W37` built four of its five fell-behind bullets on
mis-typed edges, `2026-W39` reprinted the same cluster, and `2026-W42` is the
first instance measured at three for three. That class is already recorded here
under the `interpret.md`-merged-never-deployed entries, and the standing rule is
satisfied by those.

The repeat worth recording is the grading procedure's, and this is its fourth
instance of one shape. Three are already written into
`docs/voice/canon.md`'s own grading section, each as a correction to the pass
before it: `INC-2026-09-30-standing-defect-unverified-for-three-grades` was
which COPY of the issue a grade read,
`INC-2026-10-12-the-grade-could-not-tell-an-edition-from-an-archive` was which
EDITION, and the 2026-10-10 addition was which GENERATOR. Each one was a check
that read the thing in front of it and never asked whether that thing was the
thing it was supposed to be reading. This is the fourth and it is about the
issue's SOURCES: the pass whose stated job is to ask whether what the issue says
is so has six questions, and every one of them is answered by reading the issue.
Two numbers against each other. A number against its own sentence. A claim about
alexandria's own counts. A verdict against the half of the sentence it read. Not
one of them opens a paper.

So the grade was not careless, which is the part that makes it an incident
rather than a bad run. It was complete against a procedure with no question in
it about what the papers say, on the one section the product is sold on.

consistency is cheap to check and an issue's truth is not, so the procedure grew

on the other. The one surface where that asymmetry is fatal is the section whose
whole promise is that something the field believed is no longer true.
