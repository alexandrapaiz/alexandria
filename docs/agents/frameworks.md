# The frameworks register

**Enforced at:** prompts/pm-agent.md §1e, which triages every framework into this register and kills adopted ones by default at their review date.

Maintained by the PM (charter §1e). The law, the owner's own words in
spirit: a framework must never consume more than the work it
organizes. Entries name the problem they solve; one trial at a time;
adopted practices carry ceremony cost and a kill-by-default review
date. Discarded is the most common verdict by design.

## Review, 2026-11-09 (both review dates named "at/after launch" and launch, dated Oct 13, has passed and missed)

**Working Backwards: honestly thin.** `docs/prfaq/` holds only its own
README, zero actual PR/FAQ documents, despite one qualifying initiative
this quarter (the Oct 13 launch, which is larger than a sprint item by any
reading). Extended rather than killed, because the company standard
(pm.md §2c) now mandates it org-wide regardless of this repo's own trial
verdict, but the honest local reading is that the rule exists and has not
yet been exercised once here. Review date reset to 2026-12-31 to re-check
whether the next qualifying initiative actually produces one.

**Pre-mortem: could not confirm it ran.** The board carried a task for it
(`docs/sprints/sprint-review-2026-09-18.md`) for sprint 3 / pre-launch, but
no pre-mortem document or output was found anywhere in `docs/` this run,
and three of O1's KRs around the Polar blocker it existed to catch (missed
repeatedly, flagged in check-in after check-in without ever escalating past
"noted") read exactly like the failure mode this framework is supposed to
prevent. Marked **discarded-with-reason** rather than extended: a $0, one-
hour exercise whose own output cannot be found a month later did not pay
for itself, whether because it never ran or because it ran and changed
nothing. Re-adoptable if the owner wants it tried again with its output
committed to a file this time.

## Studied, not adopted

- Shape Up (Basecamp): genuinely good, wrong clock for a weekly-cadence
  org; appetite thinking informs sprint sizing.

## Discarded, with reasons

- SAFe: the cautionary tale; enterprise ceremony that consumes the work.
- EOS/Traction: meeting-heavy for a company whose meetings are agent runs.
- Holacracy: role bureaucracy; charters already do this with less ink.
- DACI/RACI: decision matrices for ambiguous owners; every lane here has
  exactly one.
- Pre-mortem: discarded 2026-11-09 on review, see above — its own output
  could not be found a month after its one scheduled run.
