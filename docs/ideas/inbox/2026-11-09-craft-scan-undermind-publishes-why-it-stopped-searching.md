### 2026-11-09 - Craft scan: Undermind publishes why it stopped searching

- Trigger: the daily craft scan, rotating `docs/market/landscape.md`. The
  Undermind entry there is dated 2026-09-18 and says in its own last line that
  it rests on search snippets only, so it had never been observed. Read live
  at undermind.ai on 2026-11-09.
- What: the thing worth stealing is that Undermind reports the shape of its
  own search and not only its result. It prints a result line of the ordinary
  kind, `Search completed. Found 57 relevant papers and 40 PDFs`, and then
  three things beside it that this product prints nowhere. A discovery curve
  of relevant papers found over time, carrying a figure for the fraction found
  at ten minutes. A stated stopping criterion, which is that a production
  search stops on its own after about two and a half minutes. A monitoring
  flag for publications that arrive later. Taken together those tell a reader
  what the marginal return was at the moment the search stopped, which is the
  residual rule of the same morning arriving from a product rather than from a
  register. What alexandria does better is auditability of the claim rather
  than of the search. Undermind's per-paper match percentage and its evidence
  quality review are self reported and a reader cannot check either one, where
  every claim in a digest here cites the paper it came from and a provenance
  gate refuses the ones that do not. Its stopping rule is also unreproducible
  by construction, since an average duration is not a window a reader can
  name, and the arXiv window here is.
- First step: it is the entry above this one, which is why this note does not
  duplicate it. Pricing is noted for the market seat rather than acted on
  here, since it is a competitor's and not a cost: free, then $16 a month on
  annual billing for Pro, then $15 per person for Team.
