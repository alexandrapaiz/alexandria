## The keep-ratio derivation, with its one assumption named

Stated by SmartSearch (Table 1 and §3.6): 431 grep candidates on LoCoMo
against 601 total passages; 120 candidates on LongMemEval-S against 494;
default fixed budget 2,000 words yielding 3,141 average tokens; Table 4's
budget-matched pair is no-reranker 76.8 and MiniLM-L-12 84.7, both at 1,547
tokens.

Derived here, not in the paper: LoCoMo-10 conversations are ~9K tokens over
601 passages, so a passage averages ~15 tokens, so a 1,547-token budget admits
roughly 100 of 431 candidates, about a quarter. **The passage-size step is the
assumption; everything else is quoted.** The conclusion it supports is that
+7.9 pp at ~25 percent kept sits monotonically between the citing paper's
+17.4 at 10 percent and +1.5 at 67 percent, and between Fidelity Before
Structure's +2.9 and +0.6 at its stated 15 of 30.

A reader who rejects the assumption still has the paper's own statement of the
regime ("on LongMemEval-S the budget fills entirely") and the unordered-pool
confound, which needs no derivation: mean gold rank with no reranker is 195 of
431 on LoCoMo and 47 of 120 on LongMemEval-S, against 8 and 2 with the
cross-encoder.
