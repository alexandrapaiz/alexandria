# Market

The outside view, written by the market research agent
(prompts/market-agent.md) every Friday and committed by the owner's
merge. The PM agent reads the newest brief when planning Monday's
sprint; the OKR agent picks its monthly benchmark competitors from the
landscape map.

- `landscape.md` — the living competitor map: one dated entry per
  product with audience, pricing, strengths, and weaknesses against
  alexandria. Entries are added and retired with dated notes, never
  silently deleted.
- `positioning.md` — why pay for alexandria, the observed price ladder
  of paid comps, and the dated evolution of both.
- `briefs/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — one page per week: findings with sources,
  competitor moves, the clearest unmet need, and ledger proposals the
  evidence triggered.
