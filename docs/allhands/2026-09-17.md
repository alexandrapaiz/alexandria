## Decisions (owner's, binding)

1. **Pricing: the digest is FREE**, full issues, the acquisition engine
   and the human-in-the-loop interface. **The paid spine is $20/month**:
   the operational layer (skill library, claim graph access,
   automations). Annual anchoring at ten months ($200) is
   evidence-supported but not yet decided. This supersedes the $10/$30
   tiers in vision §4.
2. **Launch: Monday, October 13, 2026.** Scrum style: ship minimal,
   update continuously. The PM defines everything needed before launch
   day and guides sprint 1 of the runway.
3. **Product reframing** (recorded in vision §0): the product is the
   data layer and orchestration layer for frontier AI research and
   architecture, sold as skills, possibly a memory layer, and
   automations; the newsletter is the interface that keeps the human in
   the loop. Profitable at launch.
4. **Liberty stands** for all agents: decide everything decidable;
   reserve only money, secrets, and purpose for the owner.
5. **Differentiation** (owner, verbatim, added after dispatch): "make
   sure theres product differentiation as well; this is not necessarily
   ai news but is specifically technical; research; systems; directly
   applicable tools for orchestration essentially." alexandria does not
   compete with AI news. The identity is technical research and systems
   intelligence plus directly applicable orchestration tools. Every
   positioning artifact, feature proposal, and digest voice decision
   must hold this line; the TLDR class is an acquisition comp, not a
   category peer.
6. **Sellable-product focus: skills, for now** (owner, verbatim, after
   the seat reports): "for sellable products lets focus on skills for
   now." The engineer's other four product proposals (hosted RAG API,
   synthesis tier, harness-audit service, claim-graph API) stay in the
   ledger as later candidates, not current work; all four carry
   rejected-for-Q4 verdicts dated 2026-09-18. The composed thesis
   the seats converged on independently stands as the product story:
   evidence-backed skills, "skills with receipts," claim-graph
   citations as the headline differentiator no marketplace offers.
7. **A skill agent is commissioned** (owner approved the ExO's
   proposal on the spot): a weekly Tuesday seat owning extract to
   panel to gold (prompts/skill-agent.md, ADR-22). Its database read
   access waits on the owner's NEON_RO_URL secret.
8. **The ExO also maintains the GitHub home** (owner, 2026-09-18):
   README and docs stay truthful to the system as it is, including
   that the pipeline now includes agents; merged branches get cleaned,
   stale PRs flagged, the Projects board checked once its token
   exists. Added to the ExO charter §5b.
