## The single biggest weakness

**Half of every evidence grade the generator asks for cannot be written from
the payload, and six of the seven grades in 2026-W40 filled its position with
who ran the work. The hand-written rewrite, composed by this seat with the
rule open, made the same substitution.**

The generator says the grade has two halves: how strong the number is, and,
where the payload supports it, what the work did not measure. The payload
never supports it. `pipeline/distill.py` stores `claim`, `evidence`, `topics`
and `procedure`, and all four record something a paper did. No field anywhere
records something a paper left out. So the rule asks for an absence, supplies
a list of presences, and correctly forbids inventing one.

What arrives instead is provenance wearing a limitation's clothes. Six of
seven grades in 2026-W40 say who ran the work or who has not repeated it. One,
"no external benchmark", names a measurement that is missing. The Batch, read
live on 2026-09-19, does it in every item: "it has not provided accuracy
metrics for the classifiers that screen incoming data."

This is the axis the market wins on, and on the scored benchmark it is the
only one of six where the rewrite fails to beat the issue it rewrites.

**It does not get a seventh wording.** The charter's structure watch sends a
structural fix to the engineer rather than to the prompt, and the missing

the half a prompt can hold, which is the prohibition on substituting: when
