## 4. Law 13, and why a correct law did not bind

Worth its own section because the owner's dispatch asks for exactly this
and because the failure is instructive rather than careless.

Law 13 was written on 2026-09-24 from a struck sentence, "You spent last
week watching agents get faster by doing less at test time". The register
recorded what that sentence contained, which is a pointer at a previous
issue, and the generator's gate was built to catch four shapes of
pointer. The rehearsal's opening contains none of the four. It names no
issue, says no "last week" in the gate's sense, claims no shared history
in the first person plural, and uses no definite article standing in for
an introduction. It passed a gate that was working correctly.

What it does instead is tell the reader what their week was. The defect
was never the reference. **The defect is a past-tense verb with "you" as
its subject**, and the reference was only the form that defect happened
to take the first time she saw it.

The generator made it likelier, which is the part this seat owns. The
opening slot said, in its own words, that the greeting's "you" "says what
the reader is building and what their week has been like", and the
sentence after it narrowed the prohibition to reading: "It never says
what the reader has READ." A model obeying that file was told to write
the reader's week and told not to write the reader's reading. It did both
as instructed.

There is a third failure in the same slot and it runs the other way. The
reprint has no greeting at all, which is ban list 52, the fix by
deletion, appended on 2026-09-24. So the greeting slot has now failed
three times in three directions: absent, pointing backwards, and
narrating. All three are what L-A14 in `docs/standards/lessons.md`
predicts of a rule that forbids an outcome and never states the safe
form. The slot said what the line may not be four separate ways and never
once said what it is.

It says now. One present-tense sentence about the reader's work, then the
news, and a stranger has to enjoy the sentence. The form is written out
in the slot so that following the rule is copying rather than composing,
in a subject the payload cannot contain, and both struck specimens moved
to the stands-alone gate at the end of the file, where finished output is
read rather than written. Keeping them where the line gets written is ban
list 53 and it is how "Read these yourself" shipped.

## 5. The claims pass

The generator's rule says "name only items that actually appear below"

serious defect in either print, it is the third sentence a reader meets,
and no prose pass could catch it because the sentence is well written.
A count catches it. The links rule and the grades rule are both counted
for exactly this reason, and the contents line is not.

**The rehearsal's synthesis is false as stated.** "Three labs landed on
the same side of that question from different angles" introduces three
findings, and the second, that sequential training stages erase each
other, is not about whether a harness can be baked in. Two of the three
share a subject. The sentence claims three.

**The two prints disagree about a number's baseline.** The reprint reads
"After the search stage, single-hop search scores 45.2." The rehearsal
reads "After the math stage, single-hop search scores 45.2." Same paper,
same number, two different stages credited with producing it. One is
wrong, and nothing on either page lets a reader tell which. Flagged for
whoever holds the payload. The positive example this run wrote into canon
avoids the disputed clause and says only what both prints agree on, which
is that the collapse follows the shopping stage.

**An overturned belief is quoted with no source.** The reprint's
fell-behind item states the belief twice and differently, first as "small

then as the earlier claim that "only a few GPU-hours" were needed. The
second is in quotation marks, attributed to nobody, and carries no link.
A quoted string is the strongest form of attribution on a page and this
one has no paper behind it.

**A term arrives under a second name.** The rehearsal says single-hop
search scores 45.2 and then that "NQ gets back to 33.5", with no sentence
saying those are the same measurement. "NQ" is never expanded. This is
ban list 54, appended 2026-09-24 and enforced in the first-use pass on
