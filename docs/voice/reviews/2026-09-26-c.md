## 4. The claims pass, and the question it was missing

The pass asks whether two compared things measure the same quantity, and
whether a concession was written and then walked past. Run 14 added a third
question about alexandria's own claims. Both of the first two look outward,
from a number in the issue to a number somewhere else.

Neither looks at the issue against itself, and that is where this print
fails. The 44.3% and the 30% are not a comparison the issue makes. They are
two scores for one method, in one item, four sentences apart, and no gate in
the procedure has any reason to put them side by side. The reader does it
immediately, because the numbers are the part of an item a builder reads
first.

So the pass gains a fourth question, in the canon and as a gate in the
generator. Group every figure by the system it describes. Inside a group, two
numbers on the same named measure are either the same number, or two setups
the issue has told the reader apart in the sentence where the second one
appears. Two repairs and no third: name the setup, or print one number. An
ablation is evidence that the method did the work, not a second score for it.

**The canon's own worked example carries this defect**, and it is reported
rather than edited. The positive rewrite under law 14 reads "It finishes 44.3%
of tasks with the scaffolding gone" and then "Learning from corrected runs
scored 30%", with nothing between them to say the second is a smaller
experiment. That example is the owner's approved illustration and the laws
section changes only by her ruling. It is also the text a writer copies from,
so a defect in it propagates by design. Her call.
