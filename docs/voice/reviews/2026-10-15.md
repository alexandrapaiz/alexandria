So the requirement is complete, it is unconditional, it anticipates the exact
failure, and it shipped a print in which every pick violates it.

**Why, and the mechanism is the same shape as yesterday's four.** The step the
rule asks for is "Check each pick against the ISSUE you have just written,
section by section". That is a reading, and worse, it is a reading for an

the body's lead is a short written phrase and the list's is the paper's full
italic title.
