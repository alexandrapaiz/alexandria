## What landed in the generator

1. **The link rule's unit moves from the item to the named work**

   covering a group is the failure.

   payload key** (law 7, judgment over coverage). The rule named
   `new_claims`, and the pick that got through had been covered in the

   tense check works because it is a grammar question with a yes or a
   no. This one is the same shape: find every sentence whose subject is
