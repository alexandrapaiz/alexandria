# Four lines that claim the library read what it only ingested

**Writer seat, 2026-10-18. One candidate per surface, for her word and then
for the frontend seat's diff.**

Canon law 15 is her ruling of 2026-09-25, and verdict 6 of her site-copy
ruling of 2026-09-29 says it again for the site: never "read" for ingested.
Four reader-facing lines break it right now, on main, and three of them have
been live since the law was written.

The law fixes the words itself, which is why these are one candidate each
rather than a menu. Papers **come in**, **arrive** or **land**, and that is
ingestion. Papers are **sorted** or **ranked**, and that is triage. "Read in
full" and "read closely" are the only acts the word may name, and they cite
the full-read count and no other. So the repair is a substitution the law
already specifies, and nothing below is a judgment about voice that she has
not already given.

Every candidate is clean against `docs/voice/check_voice.py reading`, tested
line by line rather than assumed.

## 1. The home page's metric line

`site/app/page.jsx:44`

Now:

> **{papersThisWeek}** papers read this week

The candidate:

> **{papersThisWeek}** papers came in this week

The number is the week's arrivals, so the verb was the neighbouring count's.
This is the specimen the law was written from, printed on the home page: the
issue of 2026-09-26 said the library read 1,289 papers on a day when 1,289
had arrived and 164 had ever been read in full.

## 2. What the service is

`site/app/terms/page.jsx:19`

Now:

> The library reads AI research and keeps a record of what it claims, then
> writes a digest from that record and turns durable procedures into agent
> skills.

The candidate:

> AI research comes into the library, which keeps a record of what each paper
> claims, then writes a digest from that record and turns durable procedures
> into agent skills.

Two changes and both are the law. The verb goes, and "what it claims" becomes
"what each paper claims", because the record holds each paper's claims and the
pronoun had drifted onto the research as a mass.
