# Preference data: site copy, 2026-09-20 session

Every candidate the owner saw, verbatim, with her verdict and her
reason in her words. Six rounds. Built for Ursa's RLHF / taste loop
and for the writer seat. Nothing here was set on the site.

## What she said the value IS (the missing positive spec)

- The value to a BUILDER, not the mechanism: "its describing the
  mechanism not what it delivers. or the value to a builder."
- "no mention of a growing self mantaining corpus, nothing. thats my
  point." The product is a living, compounding, self-maintaining
  library of the frontier; copy that omits this has missed the point.
- Register: plain short sentences, serious, not marketing, not an ad,
  not cute, not defensive, not choppy. "how would an engineer or
  someone like sheldon get excited about a product like this and just
  describe it plainly and without much emotion", but NOT as mechanism.

## Pattern the chair sees (for ExO and Ursa, not a verdict)

Every rejected candidate is about the ISSUE or the MECHANISM. None
is about the LIBRARY: the corpus that grows every day, keeps itself
correct, and compounds. Her one positive instruction that no round
honored: the value is the living library, and the builder's value is
having it.
