## How this surface was missed

Every site-copy round this seat has run has worked from the pages a person
browses. `/llms.txt` is served from a template literal inside a route handler,
carries no heading a page audit would list, and is the one public surface
written for machines, so no round has opened it. The copy in it predates the
owner's rulings of 2026-09-25 and 2026-09-29 and has never been checked
against either.

## The four findings

**1. "Every week the library reads new AI research." Canon law 15.** The law
is the owner's ruling of 2026-09-25 and its words are fixed so that following
it is copying rather than composing. Papers come in, arrive or land. Papers
are sorted or ranked. Papers are read in full, and that is the only act the
word "read" may name, against the full-read count and no other. The same page
says it twice, in the summary line and again in the paragraph under it, "It
reads the week's AI research, breaks each paper into single claims". The
second one is worse, because "each paper" makes the blanket explicit. Her
2026-09-29 site ruling says the same thing in four words, never "read" for
ingested, on the site as in the issue.

**2 and 3. Two registered claims shipping unhedged.**
`docs/agents/quality-claims.md` names this exact file, line 30, in two rows.
"Revised when the research sharpens it" is `queued`, with zero revisions ever
fired. "Retired with an explanation when it is overturned" is `not built`,
with no field for the explanation and nothing that sets the status. The
charter's rule for both states is the same: hedged the way
`site/app/routines/page.jsx` hedges a routine that does not exist yet, or it
does not ship.

The third sentence is not in the register and is the one that does damage,
because it turns the unbuilt mechanism into an instruction:

> so the version you loaded last month may not be the one that holds today.
> Check the version before you rely on it.

Five of six skills are at version 1 and none has ever been revised by a
mechanism, so an agent that follows that advice is spending a check on a thing
that has never changed. A claim about our artifact, addressed to a reader who
cannot check it, is the shape the charter's one question is for.

**4. A date that has passed.** The page says "$20 a month. Opens October 13,
2026." Today is October 18. The page that owns the fact,
`site/app/pricing/page.jsx`, carries no date at all and renders "Opens shortly"
off a live flag. So the only dated promise on the site is on the page nobody
reviews, and the date is five days stale.

## What each change is for

- **"lands in the library" and "New papers arrive every day, get sorted, and
  the ones that earn it are read in full."** Law 15's own vocabulary, in its
  own order, so the three acts stay three acts. The sentence is longer than
  what it replaces and it is undecorated, which is her 2026-09-29 ruling.
- **"Each finding is taken out with its evidence."** Law 15 again: findings
  are taken out of the papers that were read. It also lands the one claim on
  the register that holds without qualification, that every finding carries
  its evidence.
- **"Skills will be revised ... That is still being built."** Plain future
  tense and a flat statement of what is not running, which is the form
  `routines/page.jsx` already uses in public. The promise is kept visible
  because it is the roadmap and a reader is owed it. What is removed is the
  present tense that said it already happens.
- **"every skill in the catalogue today stands at the version it was written
  at, and the version string tells you which one you have."** This replaces
  advice built on a false premise with a fact an agent can act on. It is also
  true today and will stay true after the revision job ships, because then the
  version string will start moving and the sentence still holds.
- **The date is gone, and no new one is put in.** Whether access has opened is
  not a writing question. The pricing page already answers it from a flag, so
  the one line on the site that hard-codes a date should not exist whatever
  the answer is.
