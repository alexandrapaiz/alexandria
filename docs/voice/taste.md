# The owner's writing taste register

Every editorial ruling the owner gives on the newsletter's words and
structure, recorded and permanent, exactly like docs/design/taste.md
for pixels. The writer seat reads this every run and never edits it;
the chair and the PM record her rulings.

## Standing law (predates this file)

- 2026-09-17 — The old digest read "stale and mechanical and boring";
  the voice overhaul (PR #13) exists because of that ruling.
  Benchmarking against the best newsletters is mandatory, not
  optional.
- Digest register: Morning Brew discipline with technical substance,
  simple language, why-it-matters framing.
- House prose: plain sentences, transition words, no stylistic em
  dashes, no semicolon joins, no buzzwords.

## Rulings, dated

  discovery revealed by traction (citations, adoption, replication),
  never release date. She has stated this repeatedly; a ranking that
  reads as a release-date feed violates her standing law. The daily's
  Compounding section exists because of this ruling.

  "New and unproven", and any exception argues its evidence (the
  Agora reproduction count precedent).

  greeting.
- 2026-09-19 — Evidence grading appears in-line per item.
- 2026-09-19 — Instrument honesty notes are part of the product.
- 2026-09-19 — "You didn't show me the paper": links must reach the
