# Outreach list — public evidence of fit, drafted notes

Charter boundary, restated because it matters most here: **sales
contacts no one.** Everything below is a candidate and a drafted note.
The owner decides who actually hears from her, and sends it herself,
from her own account.

Every entry's "evidence of fit" is a specific, checkable public
artifact — most already sourced in docs/market/landscape.md or
docs/market/opportunities-2026-09-18.md, two verified today by fetching
the actual thread. No entry here is a generic "AI newsletter" guess;
several candidates found via a general newsletter/podcast search this
run were dropped for being SEO-listicle aggregators (Jotform, Arize,
Feedspot-style "Best AI Newsletters 2026" roundups) with no specific,
checkable claim tying them to alexandria's actual thesis — not worth
the owner's name against unverifiable fit.

## 1. sgharlow — skillcrossroads.com

**Public evidence of fit:** posted "Show HN: Linting 216 public Claude
Code skills — 69% won't reliably trigger" (news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49744398,
2026-09-17), built a linter and ran the actual audit that found the
quality crisis alexandria's whole "skills with receipts" positioning
answers directly (cited in landscape.md and opportunities.md). This
is the single strongest-fit outreach target found this run: their
research is independent, third-party validation of the exact gap
alexandria fills.

**Drafted note (owner's voice, light edit expected):**

```
Hi — I built the linter's counterpart to what you found. Your 69%
"won't reliably trigger" number is close to the reason I started
attaching evidence to every skill in my project (alexandria):
provenance, source claims, and a recorded before/after validation,
so a skill's reliability isn't a guess. One live example:
github.com/alexandrapaiz/alexandria/blob/main/skills/harness-engineering/SKILL.md

Not pitching you anything — just wanted the person who did the actual
audit to see the response to it, and I'd genuinely value your read on
whether the provenance format is enough or still missing something.
```

**Ask, if any:** none explicit — a citation/mention in a future
skillcrossroads report would be organic upside, not the point of the
note.

trail instead of manual curation. A top commenter on that thread

generate]" — the objection alexandria's provenance framing answers
directly.

**Drafted note (owner's voice):**

Curious whether you'd see that as complementary to hand-curation or
redundant with it — genuinely asking, not pitching. Repo's public:
github.com/alexandrapaiz/alexandria

**Ask, if any:** none — peer-to-peer note, possible long-term
integration conversation (skillbay.sh listing alexandria's skills with

## Not included, and why

- **TLDR AI, The Batch, AlphaSignal, Import AI, Last Week in AI**
  (all in landscape.md) — these are the acquisition comp, not curators
  who'd plausibly feature a competitor's positioning; a pitch to them
  reads as asking a rival to promote you, not genuine fit.
- **Generic "AI newsletter" listicle sites** (DataCamp, Arize, Jotform,
  Feedspot "best of 2026" roundups, found via this run's own search) —
  no specific evidence tying any of them to alexandria's actual thesis
  beyond "they list AI newsletters." Including them would mean writing
  a "drafted note" with no real evidence of fit behind it, which the
  charter's honesty line rules out even for an internal draft.
- **The Ask HN multi-agent-observability thread**
  (news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49689454, cited in opportunities.md) —
  strong evidence of demand for exactly what alexandria's claim graph
  could become (an orchestration-pattern benchmark), but the thread's
  commenters weren't individually re-verified this run (HN rate-limited
  the fetch); listing a specific person without confirming the comment
  is theirs risks a wrong attribution. Worth a second pass next run
  once that thread can be re-checked, rather than guessing now.
