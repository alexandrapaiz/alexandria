## The email

```
Hello,

Today alexandria becomes public, and I want to be straight with you about
what that means, because you are the people who were reading this before it
was a business.

What is public is the whole record. Twelve agents, each with a written
charter in the repo, each opening its own pull requests, and me merging.
Forty-three dated decisions, with the ones I reversed left in. An
eighty-entry register of everything that has gone wrong, written by the
agents that caused it.

What is not public is a checkout. The library is $20 a month and it is not
open yet, because the payment integration is not built. I said I would launch
on this date and I am launching on this date, and I would rather hand you the
record than open a cart I have to apologise for next week. When it opens you
will hear from me once.

Three things to read, in this order.

The charters, in prompts/. Open the sales agent's first. It forbids that
agent from contacting anyone, and it drafted this email.

The failure register, docs/agents/incidents.md. My favourite entry is about
the register itself: twenty-six of its entries are numbered, and those
numbers resolve to fourteen distinct ones, because a counter in a file is not
an allocator when every agent writes on a separate branch. Incidents 24, 25
and 26 each exist three times.

The decisions, docs/decisions.md. The week the pricing changed is in there,
and so is the week I had to remake the payment choice because Stripe does not
operate in Guatemala.

Three more things you should hear from me rather than find. The archive holds
one issue, because I pulled the pilot for not being good enough to be the
first thing a stranger reads. Six skills are live and one of them carries a
recorded trial showing that loading it changed what the model recommended,
so that result is an anecdote and not yet a practice. And the register cannot
tell you which of its own incidents are open, because most entries have no
status line.

If you find something wrong in any of it, send it to me and I will register
it with your name on the entry.

The digest keeps arriving, free and in full, the way it has been.

Accelerate every builder and agent to frontier speed.

Alexandra
```

## Never in this email

- Any subscriber count, or any phrase that lets one be inferred. The list is
  comped friends and the email is written to people who know that.
- No urgency, no deadline, no discount, no founding-member price. Pricing is
  the owner's and nothing here reframes $20.
- No claim that the library opens today.
- No paper count carrying the verb "read" unless the full-read count sits
  beside the arriving count (canon law 15).
- The close is the one the 2026-09-30 ruling set for every issue and every
  email, and it is not rewritten here.

## The referral ask, and why it is one line

`../../launch/referral.md` holds the mechanics. On this send the ask is the
open register and nothing else, because the one mechanic that works on a list
of fifteen friends is asking them to find something broken. A share-this-issue
widget needs an audience this list does not have yet.
