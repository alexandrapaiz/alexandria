## Did the $0-cost-base principle hold, and what threatens it next month

It held, close to the letter: the first direct cost the books have
ever recorded is a roughly twenty-dollar annual domain registration,
which is about as close to zero as a real business can get. Two things
threaten that next month. First, Vercel Pro adds a real recurring
$20/month the moment commercial launch happens on 2026-10-13, which is
a bigger monthly number than everything booked so far combined.
Second, the Claude subscription is already a real, active cost with an
unknown size; it has sat as a named placeholder since the 2026-09-18
all-hands, and until the owner supplies the figure, this close cannot
say honestly whether the org's actual compute cost is trivial or
material. Both are named, not hidden, and neither is a surprise: they
are already forecast lines above, not new findings.
