ever recorded is a roughly twenty-dollar annual domain registration,
which is about as close to zero as a real business can get. Two things
threaten that next month. First, Vercel Pro adds a real recurring

say honestly whether the org's actual compute cost is trivial or
material. Both are named, not hidden, and neither is a surprise: they
are already forecast lines above, not new findings.
