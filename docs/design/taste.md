# The owner's taste register

Every design ruling the owner gives is recorded here and becomes
permanent. This is the accumulating human input that great UI
requires: not a model's priors, hers. The frontend seat reads this
file every run and treats each entry as law until she revises it.
The chair or the PM appends entries when she rules; the seat may
never edit them.

## Standing law (predates this file)

- Black and white only. No color enters the palette without her word.
- The hero mark's geometry, timing, and choreography are untouchable
  without her word.
- Apple-clean is the register: restraint, air, one idea per scene.
- The mission line is exactly "Accelerate every builder to frontier
  speed." and stands alone. The retired subtitle is never resurrected.
- No coming-soon pages standing in for something being sold (ADR-26).
- Never append explanatory subtitles to headings. Bare nouns; units
  and clarifications go in note slots, not in the heading.
- Copy voice: plain sentences, transition words, no stylistic em
  dashes, no semicolon joins, no buzzwords, sell the product never
  the recipe.
