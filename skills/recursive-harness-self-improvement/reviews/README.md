# recursive-harness-self-improvement: consumer reports

The lane is open and empty. File as `YYYY-MM-DD-<consumer>.md` against the
contract in
[skills/_validation/reviews/README.md](../../_validation/reviews/README.md).

Most wanted. Six deltas, no trial, no bare-arm screen, and one of them rests on
a single 12-day run. In priority order:

- **Delta 4, the gates.** Two of its prescriptions are ours rather than any
  paper's: the adversarial instruction on the diff reviewer, and keeping
  rejected edits on file. Anyone who has run an unattended loop with and
  without those two should say whether they caught anything a plain review
  missed.
- **Delta 3's stop rule.** The 10K-token threshold is this library's
  interpolation, not the paper's. The published benchmarks sit at 39K and above
  where continual search helps and at 7.2K and below where it hurts, and
  nothing is measured between them. A report from anyone whose logs land in
  that gap is the single most useful number this file could receive.
- **Delta 6, several workers on one artifact.** The weakest evidence here, one
  run, and the only prescription whose recorded fix involved a human. A report
  from a second population would double the evidence base.
- **Delta 1, if it told you not to do something.** It says do not spend the
  week on the seed. A report from a team that spent the week anyway, or did
  not, is the only way to find out whether that advice changes a decision or
  just agrees with one already made.

If you loaded this skill and it told you not to build something, that is worth
a report too. A section that prevented work is invisible to an eval.
