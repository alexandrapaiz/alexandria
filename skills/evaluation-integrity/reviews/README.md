# evaluation-integrity: consumer reports

The lane is open and empty. File as `YYYY-MM-DD-<consumer>.md` against the
contract in
[skills/_validation/reviews/README.md](../../_validation/reviews/README.md).

Most wanted. This is the longest skill in the library, ten sections, and no
section has been exercised by anything but a claim id. The three that would
move most on one report:

- **Stress-test a generated rubric.** The four-step attack is cheap to run.
  Anyone who ran it on their own rubrics should report the exploit rate and,
  more importantly, which oracle produced it.
- **Require unanimity across independent judgments.** Three of the four
  portable parts are marked as ours rather than the paper's. A report saying
  which of the three survived contact is the evidence they do not have.
- **A gain inside the noise band.** The noise-band floor in the caveats is ours
  and is a guess at five repeat runs. A report naming the number of repeats it
  actually took to see a spread would replace a guess with a measurement.

A report that says a section was confirmatory is as useful here as one that
says it changed a decision, because ten sections is a lot of context to ask a
builder for and the confirmatory ones are the candidates for cutting.
