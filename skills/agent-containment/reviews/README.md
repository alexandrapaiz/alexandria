# agent-containment: consumer reports

The lane is open and empty. File as `YYYY-MM-DD-<consumer>.md` against the
contract in
[skills/_validation/reviews/README.md](../../_validation/reviews/README.md).

This skill needs reports more than any other in the library. It is the only one
with an empty `provenance.claims` list, so its whole evidence base is six papers
read once, and its status is `draft` until its eval runs. A single report from
someone who actually shipped a boundary would be the first evidence about it
that is not a reading.

Most wanted:

- **The enforcement point, section 1.** The claim that a deny list over strings
  the agent composed is close to worthless is the file's central finding and the
  one most likely to be argued with. A report from a team that replaced a deny
  list with an interceptor should say what the migration cost.
- **The tier decision, section 5.** The numbers are one company's cluster. Any
  second data point on what a container costs against a microVM for
  repository-level agent work would matter out of proportion to its size.
- **Section 7, undo and context reconciliation.** The least standard practice
  here, and the one a builder is most likely to skip. If you skipped it, that is
  the report: say what went wrong or say nothing did.
